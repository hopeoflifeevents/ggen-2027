FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about GGEN 2027: EMPOWERED

How much is the registration fee, and what is included?

Passes start at ₱350 for Early Bird (₱500 Regular), with bundle promos available like Buy 1 Take 1 (₱600) and the 5+1 Delegation Pass (₱2,000). Every pass includes full access to all four keynote plenary sessions, unhindered praise & worship, and the official GGEN 2027 conference kit.

How do I pay via GCash, and when will I get my pass?

Select your pass tier above and tap Save GCash QR to Photos. Open your GCash app, tap QR, upload the saved image, and transfer the exact total. Attach your transaction screenshot in the form and submit. Our finance committee cross-verifies receipts within ~24 hours, and your official QR entrance pass will be delivered directly to your email inbox!

Can our youth group or church register together as a delegation?

Yes! You can register up to 6 delegates in a single submission using the delegate stepper or by selecting promo bundles like Buy 1 Take 1 or 5+1 Barkada Pass. You only need to make one consolidated GCash payment, and individual admission passes will be generated and emailed for each registered member.

What time do doors open on August 30, 2027?

Delegate check-in booths open at 7:30 AM PHT at JIA CMNV (Jesus Is Alive Community Metro North, Quezon City). The opening worship rally and morning keynote plenary session commence promptly at 9:00 AM. Arrive early to scan your pass smoothly and claim your seat.

What is the dress code, and what should I bring?

Modest casual or conference streetwear suitable for dynamic worship and movement. Bring your smartphone with your official QR entrance pass (saving a screenshot to your photo gallery is recommended for quick gate scanning), a physical or digital Bible, a notebook, and an expectant heart!

Can I transfer my pass if I am unable to attend?

Passes are non-refundable but fully transferable. If you need to reassign your pass to a friend or churchmate, simply contact our logistics team at hopeoflifeevents@gmail.com with your Registration ID at least 5 days prior to the conference to re-issue the pass in their name.
