FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is there a registration fee for GGEN 2027?

Admission is free, but all delegates must register through this official portal to receive an authorized pass due to venue seating limitations at JIA CMNV.

What is the dress code for the conference?

Modest casual or conference streetwear. Wear comfortable clothes suitable for worship and active breakout sessions.

Can our youth group or church register as a delegation?

Yes! Have each member of your youth group submit this form individually using the exact same Church / Campus / Organization name so our verification team can group your passes together.

What time do the doors open on August 30, 2027?

Delegate check-in booths open at 7:30 AM at JIA CMNV. The morning plenary and opening worship session commence at 9:00 AM sharp.
